Meet the team

Two founders, one idea that wouldn’t leave.

A business trip to Bali, one bad wipeout, and a café that had built its whole menu on a flour we had never heard of. We came home with no recipes and one question we could not put back down: why isn’t bread at home like this? The Green Banana is our answer, baked fresh here in Santa Barbara.

Who’s behind it

The two of us.

Co-founder · CEO

Milo Stewart

Milo built his foundation as a high-performance athlete on the University of Southern California football team, then spent years as a top trainer at one of the world’s premier health clubs, coaching performance and nutrition. None of it prepared him for what a water bug could do. When he could barely eat, the first thing he kept down was green banana bread from a small café in Bali. That first bite became breakfast, lunch and dinner there for the rest of the trip.

Runs the permits, the recipes, and the part nobody romanticises — getting up at five to actually bake the thing.

Santa Barbara, CA
Co-founder · CTO

Erol Morkoç

At 13, Erol was diagnosed with a cancer so rare and so aggressive that the treatment that saved his life was a last-ditch therapy developed at the university he would later attend. He came out of it grateful, and has never once complained about the lifelong sensitivity to gluten and dairy it left him with. In that café in Bali he noticed something new: after a big meal, his stomach wasn’t throbbing and his skin wasn’t swollen. That was the moment the idea became a company. Today he handles production, sourcing, and everything that has to work the same way twice.

If a batch is consistent, that’s him.

How it started

Somebody planted an idea in us. We’re passing it on.

It was a business trip to Bali that felt more like play, until a wipeout cost Milo four days and nearly 35 pounds. That is how we met the food that changed our lives.

In the United States, green banana flour is still barely known. In much of the world it is an everyday superfood — stirred into water, or milled fine and worked into a dough as a base for bread. In Bali we found a café that had built an entire menu on it: sourdough, pizza, pancakes, waffles, all from one flour, and none of it tasting like a compromise.

For Milo, it was the first food that stayed down, and it carried him through his recovery. For Erol, it was the first big meal in years that didn’t leave him paying for it afterward. We ate there for breakfast, lunch and dinner.

We did not come home with recipes. We came home knowing it was possible, and then spent a long time working out how — badly at first. Everything we sell is our own, developed and baked fresh here in Santa Barbara. What that café gave us was the proof, and we would not have started without it.

Then there was the other half of it: coming back to a country where the default loaf carries thirty ingredients and a marketing budget, and not being able to unsee that. No gap in the market, no business plan. An idea that got in and would not come out.

Sorry. Now it’s in you too.

What we stand for

Food that’s worth the trouble.

Function over marketing

Green banana flour is genuinely rich in resistant starch — and we say plainly that baking reduces it, because it does. We publish the figures for the flour, the caveat for the loaf, and the source for both. Nobody has to take our word for anything.

Less waste

Green bananas are picked before they ripen, which turns fruit that often never reaches a shelf into flour. Reducing food waste isn’t a side note for us — it’s built into the ingredient.

Local, honestly

The banana isn’t from Santa Barbara and we’ll never pretend it is. The baking is. We back local farming where it’s real, and say plainly where an ingredient travelled from.

Come say hello.

We bake a small batch most mornings and hand it out somewhere around Isla Vista, Goleta and Santa Barbara. Easiest way to meet us is to turn up.
