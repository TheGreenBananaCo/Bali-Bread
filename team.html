How it started

Why Bali Bread?

The idea started years ago in Bali, where we first encountered green banana flour and realized we’d found an ingredient worth paying attention to.

We came home curious and started experimenting. What began as an unusual flour became years of work figuring out how to make genuinely good bread with it.

Today, that bread is baked fresh in Santa Barbara.

That’s Bali Bread.

What we stand for

Food that’s worth the trouble.

Function over marketing

Green banana flour is genuinely rich in resistant starch. Baking reduces it, but doesn’t erase it. We care about what the ingredient actually does — not inflating the numbers to make a better headline.

Baked here.

The green bananas come from where bananas grow. Bali Bread is made here in Santa Barbara.

Come say hello.

We bake small batches every Sunday and Wednesday for Isla Vista, Goleta and Santa Barbara. Easiest way to meet us is to pick up a loaf.
