Why green banana flour?

Green (unripe) bananas are dried and milled before they ripen, which locks in resistant starch instead of letting it convert to sugar. The result is a mild, subtly sweet flour that bakes up light, is naturally gluten-friendly, and is easier on blood sugar than most refined flours — without tasting "healthy" for the sake of it.

We bake everything in small batches and deliver it fresh, on a schedule that fits actual college and family life: no big freezer stockpiles, no minimum orders you don't need, just bread that shows up when you expect it.

Frequently asked questions

Is green banana flour gluten-free?

Green banana flour itself is naturally gluten-free. Some of our loaves are blended with wheat flour for texture — check each product's label. A fully gluten-free loaf option can be added here once you confirm your recipes and kitchen cross-contamination setup.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?

Yes — pause, skip a delivery, change your plan, or cancel anytime from your confirmation email or by contacting us directly. (Self-service plan management can be added once real payments are connected — see the launch guide.)
